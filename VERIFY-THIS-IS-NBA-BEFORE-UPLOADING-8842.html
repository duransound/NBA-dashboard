Methodology & sources

  • "Points Added per 100" = BPM (Box Plus/Minus). RAPTOR (FiveThirtyEight) measured points contributed per 100 possessions vs. a league-average player using box scores + tracking + on/off data; it was discontinued and never released in full public form. BPM uses the identical definition and units, built from box scores alone — the closest real, sourced stand-in available. RAPTOR definition, NBAstuffer.
  • Coverage: 480 players — essentially the full league that logged real minutes this season, including deep bench and rotation players, not just stars. A handful of players with only single-digit total minutes across the season were left out.
  • Salaries: confirmed for 126 of the 480 players (Spotrac.com, full team cap tables); the rest show real impact numbers with no salary attached, so they appear on the Leaderboard but not the Value tab.
  • Players who changed teams mid-season show combined season stats under a "Traded (2/3/4 teams)" tag instead of one specific team, since that's what the underlying stats reflect. James Harden is the one exception — his stats and salary are both scoped to his time with Cleveland only, to match his prorated salary figure.
  • Source for all production stats (BPM, Win Shares, VORP, position): Basketball-Reference, 2025-26 Advanced Stats.
  • BPM is a box-score estimate, not a perfect measure — it can undervalue defense-only role players and overvalue high-usage scorers on bad teams. Treat it as one strong lens, not a verdict.
